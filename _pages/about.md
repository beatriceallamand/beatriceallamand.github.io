I am a PhD student in Economics at the **University of California, San Diego**.

My research interests are in **macroeconomics**, **international trade**, **labor**, and **development**, with a focus on **heterogeneous-agent models** and policy-relevant questions. My work combines quantitative macroeconomic theory with applied questions related to growth, labor markets, and inequality.

## Research

**Capital Flows and the Geography of Growth**  
Work in progress studying how capital mobility interacts with spatial productivity differences to shape growth and capital allocation across regions.

**Childcare as a Mobility Friction** (with Regina Calles and Grace Alster)  
Project analyzing how childcare constraints affect labor mobility, sorting, and inequality, with implications for labor market policy.

**Endogenous Entrepreneurship in HANK** (with Aruzhan Nurlankul)  
Heterogeneous-agent macro model with occupational choice, studying entrepreneurship, risk, and aggregate dynamics.

**Amplification with High-MPC Workers**  
Third-year paper studying amplification mechanisms in economies with heterogeneous marginal propensities to consume.
