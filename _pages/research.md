## Work in Progress

**Amplification with High-MPC Workers**  
Third-year paper studying amplification mechanisms in economies with heterogeneous marginal propensities to consume and endogenous labor supply.

**Childcare as a Mobility Friction** (with Regina Calles and Grace Alster)  
Project analyzing how childcare constraints affect labor mobility, sorting, and inequality, with implications for labor market policy.

**Robust Statistics for Monetary Non-Neutrality in Menu Cost Economies** (with Juan Herreño and Noémie Pinardon-Touati)

**Endogenous Entrepreneurship in HANK** (with Aruzhan Nurlankul)  
Heterogeneous-agent macro model with occupational choice, studying entrepreneurship, risk, and aggregate dynamics.
