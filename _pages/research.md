My research focuses on **Macroeconomics**, specifically exploring how capital mobility, labor market frictions, and heterogeneous agents impact aggregate economic dynamics.

## Works in Progress

### **Capital Flows and the Geography of Growth**
*Individual Project* This project studies how capital mobility interacts with spatial productivity differences to shape growth and capital allocation across regions. It examines the long-run implications of financial integration in a geographically diverse economy.

### **Childcare as a Mobility Friction**
*with Regina Calles and Grace Alster* We analyze how childcare constraints affect labor mobility, sorting, and inequality. The project provides a framework for understanding how localized domestic constraints influence broader labor market policy outcomes.

### **Endogenous Entrepreneurship in HANK**
*with Aruzhan Nurlankul* A heterogeneous-agent macro model with occupational choice. We study the intersection of entrepreneurship and risk, focusing on how individual-level uncertainty scales up to affect aggregate dynamics.

### **Amplification with High-MPC Workers**
*Third-year Paper* This paper investigates amplification mechanisms in economies characterized by heterogeneous marginal propensities to consume (MPC) and endogenous labor supply.
